# Cadence

Cadence sells video courses to learners. This glossary fixes the words the app, its emails, and
its code use for the same concepts.

## Language

### Getting money back

**Refund Request**:
A learner's ask to get their money back for a Purchase, reviewed by hand by staff, and either
approved or declined. When approved, it ends the Purchase and the learner loses access to the
course.
_Avoid_: Reversal, Chargeback, cancellation, Refund (for the request itself)

**Refund**:
The money returned to a learner when their Refund Request is approved. It is always the amount
the learner actually paid for the Purchase. A declined Refund Request produces no Refund.
_Avoid_: Reversal, Chargeback

**Refund Window**:
The 14 calendar days after a Purchase during which a Refund Request can be made, counted in whole
days and including all of the 14th day.
_Avoid_: cancel window, cancellation period

**Watched Time**:
How much of a course a learner has viewed: the furthest point reached in each lesson, summed
across lessons. Rewatching adds nothing; skipping ahead counts.
_Avoid_: watch time, viewing hours

**Chargeback**:
A payment dispute that a cardholder raises through their bank, which pulls the money back without
our agreement. It is a separate event from a Refund Request, and the app does not handle it yet.
_Avoid_: Refund, Refund Request
